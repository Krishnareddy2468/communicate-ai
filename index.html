    Our Mission

    COMMUNICATE AI is an innovation project of SRI PRATIBHA HIGH SCHOOL that helps students and people with speech, hearing or language difficulties express themselves with confidence — in English, తెలుగు and हिन्दी.

    We believe that no one should be left out of a conversation. Every voice deserves to be heard.

    What it does

    🗣️ Talk BoardPicture cards that speak everyday needs, feelings and classroom phrases.
    ⌨️ Type to SpeakType any message and hear it aloud, with smart word suggestions.
    👂 Live CaptionsTurns nearby speech into large on-screen text.
    🆘 Quick HelpOne-tap emergency messages for teachers and staff.
    🌐 Three languagesEnglish, Telugu and Hindi speech and labels.
    📴 Works offlineInstall once and use it anywhere in school.

    Install on your phone

    Android (Chrome): tap Install when prompted, or open the ⋮ menu → Install app.

    iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
